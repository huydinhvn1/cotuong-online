User Data Deletion

Last updated: October 3, 2026 · Tiếng Việt

If you signed in to Cờ Tướng Online (Xiangqi Online) with Google, we store: the provider (Google), your Google account identifier, your display name, profile picture URL, creation date, Win/Loss/Draw statistics, Elo ratings, the private messages you sent / received and your block list (details in the Privacy Policy). Guests who never signed in have no data to delete in our database.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to huydinhvn1@gmail.com with the subject “Cờ Tướng Online data deletion”.
  2. In the email, include your in-game display name and your account ID if available (shown in the box below while you are signed in).
  3. We will delete all of your account data (name, avatar, identifiers, statistics, private messages, block list) from our database within 30 days and confirm by email.

Revoking the app’s access

  • Google: go to myaccount.google.com/connections → choose “Cờ Tướng Online” → Delete all connections.

Removing the app only stops sharing your profile from then on; to delete data that is already stored, send the email described above.

Data on your device

Click “Sign out” in the lobby to delete the sign-in cookie. To remove the settings saved in your browser (the name you typed, sound, language, games against the computer…), clear this site’s data in your browser settings.
